Method, assumptions and limits

Scheduler. Tasks are processed by priority, then start time. For each task every available EV is scored by an exhaustive search over (station, start slot) for the cheapest feasible charge; the lowest-cost EV wins and ties follow vehicle order. Critical coverage comes first, then charging plus deadhead cost; unnecessary charging is avoided by charging only to required SOC + 5%. Constraints: SOC at departure ≥ trip energy + reserve (20%), one task per EV, chargers per station per 30-min slot, offline stations excluded, charge ends ≥10 min before departure. EVs below 30% SOC with no task get a reserve top-up to 45%.

This is a deterministic, reproducible heuristic, not a proven-optimal MIP or OR-Tools solution. Tags: F fact from the seeded simulation, C calculation, A assumption (price curve, charger speeds, 0.6 km per map unit, CO₂ factors 0.17 kg/km ICE vs 0.71 kg/kWh grid).
